Questions

What does AI Lab Consulting do?

We help banks, credit unions, insurers and fintechs put AI agents into production with the approvals and records that examiners and auditors expect.

What is an AI governance assessment?

A fixed-scope review of the AI agents you run: what each one can do and who approves its actions. You get the gaps, mapped to SR 11-7, SOX, DORA and the EU AI Act, and a ranked plan to close them.

How do you stop an AI agent from taking actions nobody approved?

Consequential actions need a signed approval from a named person, tied to the exact parameters. The system that runs the action checks the approval first and refuses anything that does not match.

Do you provide legal or compliance sign-off?

No. We design and build the controls and the evidence. Your compliance team, counsel and auditors make the legal calls.

Do you work outside financial services?

Yes. We also build machine learning and computer vision systems, including for manufacturing and retail.

Where are you based?

Laguna Beach, California. We work with clients remotely.

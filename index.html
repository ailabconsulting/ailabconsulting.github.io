Services

AI governance assessment
A fixed-scope review of the AI agents you run. You get the gaps against SR 11-7, SOX, DORA and the EU AI Act and a ranked plan to close them.
Approval and audit architecture
Permissions, per-action approvals and a decision record for each agent, enforced where the action runs.
Compliant AI-assisted engineering
Pipeline and review rules that let engineers use AI coding tools and keep the change-control evidence SOX, PCI DSS and DORA require.
Fractional AI governance lead
Part-time senior leadership for AI policy, architecture review and board reporting.

We also build agent platforms, LLM applications and machine learning systems, including for manufacturing and retail.

How engagements work

Engagements start with a fixed-scope assessment of your agents and controls. If you continue, we build the controls with your team on one workflow first, then extend them and hand over the documentation and evidence. You can stop after any step.

A fractional AI architect and governance lead is a senior person on retainer who sets your AI controls, reviews the architecture and reports to the board. Mid-sized banks, credit unions, insurers and fintechs face much the same AI expectations as large institutions, with far smaller teams and budgets.

## Policy and engineering

The role covers AI policy, control frameworks and conversations with regulators, as well as agent platform architecture (Kubernetes, MCP tool routing, LangGraph orchestration). Decisions about AI risk are made by someone who understands the policy and its engineering consequences.

## Background

We bring senior enterprise architecture and compliance experience from regulated production environments, and design with examinations and production load in mind.

## How the retainer works

You get an agreed amount of time each month, a seat at the governance meetings where AI decisions are made, and someone to call when an AI risk question comes up between meetings. The scope can extend to hands-on assessment, architecture or engineering work when needed.

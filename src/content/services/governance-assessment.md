An agentic AI governance assessment answers one question about a defined set of AI agents: if an examiner asked what an agent did and who approved it, could you show them? For many firms the honest answer today is "partly". Agents arrived faster than the controls around them.

## What we look at

We list the agents and AI-assisted workflows in scope, including the data they read and the tools they can call. For each action with consequences we trace who approves it and what gets recorded, then try to reconstruct a sample of past decisions from those records.

## How findings are mapped

Each finding is tied to controls your examiners and auditors already test: SR 11-7 for model risk, SOX for financial reporting, DORA for ICT risk in EU entities, and the EU AI Act. PCI DSS is added where card data is involved.

## What you get at the end

A ranked list of gaps, a remediation roadmap with effort estimates, and a short summary for the board or risk committee. If you continue with further work, whether with us or with your own teams, the roadmap is the starting brief.

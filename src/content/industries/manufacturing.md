In manufacturing, AI earns its keep on problems that repeat many times a day, such as unplanned stops and defects that reach customers. The usual tools are machine learning on sensor and maintenance data, computer vision on the line, and language models over technical documentation.

Most plants already collect the data these need. A first project is typically a proof of concept on one line or one class of equipment, measured against current downtime or scrap rates before it is extended.

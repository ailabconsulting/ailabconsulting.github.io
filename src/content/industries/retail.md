Most retail AI comes down to forecasting demand and setting prices well, across more products and stores than a planning team can handle by hand. Generative AI has added product content and customer conversations to that list.

Retailers hold a lot of usable data in transactions, inventory, web behavior and product catalogs. We usually start with whichever decision ties up the most money, often inventory or pricing, and test the improvement on historical data before anything goes live.
